Advising

Between Uber and Cove, I spent two years primarily investing and advising seed stage, series A, and series B startups. These days I’m focused on Microsoft AI and Tradewinds School, but I still do occasional advising and angel investing in special cases.

I’ve advised these seed-stage, series A, and series B startups on design and product:

  • Regrello — Asana for manufacturing supply chains (acq. Salesforce)
  • Pepper — Connecting food suppliers with restaurants (now Series C)
  • Office Hours — Book time with knowledge professionals from around the world
  • Doorstead — Opendoor for rentals
  • Cottage — Solving housing affordability by making it easy to build an ADU (acq. RenoFi)
  • The Pill Club — Helping women access affordable birth control and more
  • Mighty Health — Personal coach for exercise, nutrition and wellness
  • Flexspace — On demand marketplace for co-working space
  • Flowly — VR and biofeedback for pain and anxiety

Side Projects

Osito 2023–2024

With a team of three, I self-funded and built an AI-powered travel booking tool. It generated a small amount of revenue before I abandoned it to work on Cove.

Onion Map 2020

An experimental map that deemphasizes streets and highlights the relationship between topography and neighborhood names, built with OpenStreetMap data and a bespoke Mapbox style.

Degen Blues 2022

An experiment in generative music and smart contracts.

Zoom Backgrounds for Biden 2020

Digital paintings made as Zoom backgrounds in early Covid—started as a gift for friends and grew into a commission model that raised $10,000 for the Biden campaign.

Sutro Tower Inc.

An oil painting showing the TV and radio antennas on the tower—one of my favorite pieces on canvas.
